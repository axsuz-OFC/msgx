Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create a broadcast list?
Go to Menu → Broadcast lists → Enter name → Select recipients → Create.
How do I change my wallpaper?
Go to Settings → Wallpaper → Choose Image or Video.
How do I link a device?
Go to Settings → Linked Devices → Link New Device.
How do I star a message?
Long press a message → Tap Star Message.
